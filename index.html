About Kumar & Co

Field crew — median works, NH corridor

Civil engineers and builders, working on the roads you drive every day.

Kumar & Co was set up in 2008 by a site engineer who had already spent six years learning the trade at one of Coimbatore's leading civil engineering firms. Since then we've carried out highway, industrial and civil works for corporate clients, multinational groups, textile and industrial units, and residential clients across Tamil Nadu — backed today by over 17 years of combined field experience.

Our work spans national highway maintenance and road structure work, internal and external bitumen and concrete work, civil construction, commercial buildings and PEB structures for industrial and multinational facilities — all delivered under quality control, strict safety compliance and competitive, transparent rates.
