About Kumar & Co

Every great structure starts with bare ground and a plan.

Kumar & Co was set up in 2008 by a site engineer who had already spent six years learning the trade at one of Coimbatore's leading civil engineering firms. Since then we have carried out highway, industrial and civil works for corporate clients, multinational groups, textile and industrial units and residential clients across Tamil Nadu.
